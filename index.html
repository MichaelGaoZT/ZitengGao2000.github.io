Ph.D. student · Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen

Hi there! I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Shuo Yang in the iLearn-Lab.

Previously, I was a machine learning engineer at XPENG Motors, working on end-to-end autonomous driving, including imitation learning, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning fine-tuning. I received my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the National University of Singapore and my B.Eng. in Mechatronic Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology.

My research interests lie in embodied AI and autonomous driving, with a focus on vision-language-action models, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning for locomotion, manipulation, and end-to-end planning.

I am always happy to discuss research and potential collaborations. Feel free to get in touch!
